Offward

App and website blocking, focus timers and local progress tracking for iPhone.

Free version

Three focus sessions per day, a basic 25-minute timer, 5-minute breaks, start/pause/reset controls and session history. Breaks do not count towards the daily focus allowance. A session already running is always allowed to finish.

Offward Pro

A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited sessions, custom durations and saved presets, advanced statistics, Home Screen widgets, themes, background sounds and audio imports, scheduled routines, strict mode and daily goals. The intended UK price is £4.99; Apple displays the localized price before purchase. No subscription or recurring charge. App Store availability may be limited during beta testing.

Restore Pro

Open Settings → Unlock Offward Pro → Restore, using the same Apple Account as the original purchase. Apple handles payments and refunds. Free users can close the paywall to use the basic timer; it appears again when the app is reopened. Pro owners do not receive the launch paywall.

App blocking

Allow Screen Time access, select apps and websites, then start a focus session. Pausing a non-strict timer pauses its manual app blocks. Scheduled routines may still block apps during a pause. Strict sessions cannot be paused, reset or ended early inside Offward.

Widgets

With Pro unlocked, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Offward and choose a size.

Get help

Report a problem or ask a question. Include your iOS and Offward version. Issues are public; omit private or sensitive information.

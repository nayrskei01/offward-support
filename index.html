Offward

Make room for focus. Block distracting apps and websites, set routines, and track time spent focusing.

App blocking

On your iPhone, allow Screen Time access, select apps and websites, then start a focus session. Keep Screen Time access enabled. Scheduled routines can keep their apps blocked after a manual session ends.

Supporter purchase

Supporter status is an optional monthly or yearly subscription. All current focus tools are free. Apple shows the price and billing period before you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in Apple Account settings. Restore from Settings → Support Offward using the same Apple Account. Purchases may be unavailable during beta testing.

Get help

Report a problem or ask a question. Include your iOS and Offward version. Issues are public, so omit private or sensitive information.

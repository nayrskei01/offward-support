Offward

App and website blocking, focus timers and local progress tracking for iPhone.

Free version

Focus and break timers, custom durations, routines, strict mode, sounds, daily goals, app and website blocking, and your session history remain available without Pro. App blocking requires Screen Time permission on a physical iPhone.

Offward Pro

Both plans unlock Rainy Pond, pond growth rewards earned through completed focus, and advanced focus insights. Choose a monthly auto-renewing subscription or lifetime access through a one-time non-consumable purchase. Current UK prices are £1.99 per month or £24.99 lifetime. Eligible monthly subscribers can receive a 14-day free trial, then £1.99 per month unless cancelled. Apple displays the current price for your storefront before you confirm. Other countries use localized prices. Availability and introductory-offer eligibility are determined by the App Store.

Restore Pro

Open Settings → Offward Pro → Restore using the Apple Account that owns your purchase. Apple handles payments and refunds. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscriptions settings. Lifetime access has no recurring charge. The Pro screen is optional and can be closed to continue using free features.

App blocking

Allow Screen Time access, select apps and websites, then start a focus session. A non-strict session can be ended through an intentional pause. Strict sessions cannot be ended early inside Offward. Scheduled routines may retain their own blocks after a manual session ends. iOS allows an adult to revoke Screen Time permission or remove the app.

Lock Screen timer

Enable the Lock Screen timer in Offward Settings to follow your session with Live Activities on supported devices.

Get help

Report a problem or ask a question. Include your iOS and Offward version. Issues are public; omit private or sensitive information.

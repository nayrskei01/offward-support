Offward support

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026. Offward is developed by Ryan Betache.

Your data stays on your device

Offward stores your focus history, routines, preferences, daily goals and private Screen Time selections locally. It does not operate a backend, create user accounts, sell data, or include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Custom background audio you import is copied into the app’s local storage and is not uploaded.

Screen Time access

With your permission, Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks let Offward block the apps, categories and websites you select. Apple provides private selection tokens. These tokens and focus settings are shared locally with Offward’s blocking extensions using an App Group. They are not sent to the developer.

Notifications and Lock Screen timer

Optional local notifications remind you when sessions end. Optional Live Activities show your focus countdown on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. You can turn these off in Offward or iOS Settings.

Purchases

Apple processes optional supporter purchases. Offward uses StoreKit to check purchase and refund status and restore supporter status. The developer does not receive your card details. Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local history and settings remain until you delete them or remove Offward. You may delete imported audio in Offward. You may revoke Screen Time, notification or Live Activity permissions in iOS Settings. Purchase records held by Apple remain subject to Apple’s retention policies. Device backups, if enabled, are controlled by your device and backup settings.

Support and external links

If you choose to contact support, we use only the information you send to answer your request. Please do not send private Screen Time selections or sensitive information. GitHub hosts this policy and support page and may process technical request information under its privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For questions or deletion of support correspondence, contact Ryan through Offward support. Public issues are visible to others. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
